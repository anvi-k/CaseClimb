This record distinguishes research, local tests, live provider probes, and deployed behavior. The user authorized backend/provider setup, AWS deployment, and Git publication. ## Delivered behavior

CaseClimb provides a FastAPI/Mangum backend, faculty Patient Studio, ten-minute student encounters, progressive chart disclosure, authored nondisclosure and respectful clarification, event logs, and evidence-linked formative feedback. File and DynamoDB stores use revision checks and atomic provider reservations. Published versions are immutable. Patient replies come from authored case text; optional Bedrock selects only allowed identifiers. Unknown findings remain unknown. Simulated rapport is separate from assessment of empathy, competence, or alliance.

The browser includes draft editing, structural checks, reviewer attestation, patient mood and voice controls, a conversation beside a chart, captions, a timer, optional audio, consent-based dictation with manual confirmation, and a debrief. Claude supplied a bounded, read-only critique of sanitized screenshots and frontend source. Its recommendations informed the paper/ink/teal design, readable notices, compact encounter layout, feedback handoff, presentation labels, and Studio publishing workflow. Claude did not change code, inspect credentials, deploy, or independently verify production.

The shared entrance password creates a revocable four-hour session with an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookie. App assets, APIs, and documentation require it. Faculty authorization, student practice codes, and encounter-scoped tokens remain separate. Rate limits, request-size limits, provider quotas, timeouts, and idempotency constrain the prototype. This is shared demo access, not institutional identity or independent verification of faculty qualifications.
