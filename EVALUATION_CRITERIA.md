# Rutgers Health Hack 2026 - Evaluation Criteria

## Scoring Scale: 1-5

### 1. Innovation & Creativity
**How unique and original is the project?**

- **1** — Similar solutions already exist; little differentiation.
- **3** — Includes some unique or creative elements.
- **5** — Highly novel approach with clear differentiation.

### 2. Technical Implementation
**Quality of code, technical execution, cloud architecture, and data integration.**

- **1** — Little or no working code or technical implementation.
- **3** — Partial implementation; some code or technical components work.
- **5** — Robust implementation with effective use of data, integrations, and/or cloud architecture.

### 3. Healthcare Impact
**Relevance to a healthcare need and potential real-world impact.**

- **1** — Limited connection to a healthcare need or negligible potential impact.
- **3** — Addresses a relevant need with reasonable potential benefit.
- **5** — Addresses an important healthcare need with strong potential for meaningful impact.

### 4. Presentation & Demonstration
**Clarity of the presentation, demo, and supporting documentation.**

- **1** — Purpose, demo, or documentation is difficult to follow.
- **3** — Generally understandable, with some gaps in clarity.
- **5** — Clear, well-organized presentation and demo communicate the solution effectively.

### 5. Feasibility & Scalability
**Practicality of deployment in a real healthcare setting and potential to scale.**

- **1** — Major barriers to deployment or scaling.
- **3** — Feasible with moderate additional work or resources.
- **5** — Practical path to deployment and strong potential to scale.
